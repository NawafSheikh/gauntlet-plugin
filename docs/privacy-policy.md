# Gauntlet privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Gauntlet is a phone, watch and computer companion for AI coding agents
(such as Claude Code and Codex). It is made by Nawaf Sheikh
(nawafsheikh1@gmail.com).

## The short version

Gauntlet has no servers. It does not collect, store, sell or share your
data. Your phone and watch talk only to the computers you pair them with,
over your own network, encrypted.

## What stays on your devices

- **Pairing.** Your phone or watch and your computer exchange certificates
  once, when you approve the pairing on both screens. Each device keeps the
  other's fingerprint and the addresses it was reached at, on that device.
- **Sessions, questions, approvals, todos and usage.** The Gauntlet agent on
  your computer reads your AI agent sessions there and sends their names,
  states, questions, todos and plan usage straight to your paired phone and
  watch over an encrypted connection (TLS). Nothing goes through any server
  of ours; there is none.
- **Settings and the last known state** are stored on each device so the
  widget, wallpaper and watch face can show them while offline.

## Microphone and speech

When you tap the mic, Gauntlet uses your device's speech recognition to turn
what you say into text, which it sends to the session you picked on your
computer. Gauntlet asks for the on-device recognizer first. If your device
has no on-device recognizer for your language, Android's default speech
service handles it, which may process the audio under that provider's own
privacy policy (for example Google's). Gauntlet itself never records, stores
or uploads audio.

## Network and permissions

- **Local network and internet permission:** only to find and connect to your
  own paired computers (on your Wi-Fi, or your own Tailscale network if you
  use one).
- **Microphone:** only while you are speaking to a session.
- **Notifications:** to tell you when a session needs you.
- **Bluetooth (watch):** for the optional mouse and keyboard mode with your
  computer.
- **Sensors (watch):** to recognise gestures; processed on the watch.

## Children

Gauntlet is a developer tool and is not directed at children.
